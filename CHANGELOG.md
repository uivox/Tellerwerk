# Changes

What changed in each release, newest first, in plain words. Every push to `main` is a release (`1.0.<build number>`), and the files are on the [Releases](https://github.com/uivox/Tellerwerk/releases) page. A pull request with a change people will notice adds a line under *Unreleased*. Once it's merged and released, the next pull request moves those lines under the new version.

## Unreleased

## 1.0.17 (8 November 2026)

- Bakery chains: items from Ditsch, Kamps and Hofpfisterei, with the values each chain publishes.
- The offline product pack now keeps products without a barcode, such as a bakery's own items. You find them by searching.

## 1.0.15 (8 November 2026)

- *Your data* shows how much space Tellerwerk takes in total, with a size on every row.

## 1.0.14 (7 November 2026)

- The offline product pack comes in two versions: *German products only* (smaller, the default for new downloads) or *all products sold in Germany*. A pack you already have stays until you switch, and *How this is chosen* explains the rules.

## 1.0.12 (7 November 2026)

- *Not on Open Food Facts yet* (Profile → Open Food Facts) lists your own foods that Open Food Facts doesn't have, or has with different values. Nothing is looked up until you tap *Check*, and you can send each food from the list.

## 1.0.11 (5 November 2026)

- Search: *Show more* in every section, and *Load more* for Open Food Facts results.
- The *Recent* tab shows every food you've ever logged, with how often and when you last logged it. You can filter the list and remove foods from it; your diary isn't touched.

## 1.0.10 (4 November 2026)

- Restaurant items: the menus of McDonald’s, Burger King, KFC, Subway, Five Guys, NORDSEE, Starbucks and Domino’s (pre-packed items only) in Germany, from each chain's own published values. You can switch them off under Layout.

## 1.0.9 (4 November 2026)

- OpenPlate is now **Tellerwerk**: a new app with a new web address, <https://tellerwerk-app.github.io/>. To keep your data, export it in OpenPlate and import it in Tellerwerk; the README explains how. The last OpenPlate version only shows these steps.

## 1.0.8 (31 October 2026)

- New app icon: Sprout's bowl in four faces and six colours, picked under Appearance.

## 1.0.6 (29 October 2026)

- Goal colours follow your goal: when gaining or maintaining, eating more than planned is no longer shown in red.
- The streak counter celebrates your first entry of the day. It follows Motion, and it can be switched off.
- *Your usual breakfast?*: one tap adds a meal you've logged on at least 3 of the last 14 days.
- A weekly goal check-in suggests a new calorie goal from your intake and weight trend, and shows the calculation. It never changes your goal by itself, and every change can be undone.

## 1.0.5 (29 October 2026)

- The web app starts faster, with a start screen while it loads.

## 1.0.4 (28 October 2026)

- *Your data* (Profile) shows what is stored on your device and every server the app can talk to.
- A connection log lists the last 7 days of connections, never their contents. It can be switched off.
- App lock with Face ID, fingerprint or the device PIN; in the browser, with a passkey.
- Backups can be encrypted with a password.
- CSV export of the diary, weights, water, fasting and your own foods.

## 1.0.3 (28 October 2026)

- Send your own foods to Open Food Facts, signed in with your own Open Food Facts account. You see exactly what is sent first, and nothing is sent without a tap.

## 1.0.2 (23 October 2026)

- An amount wheel on the food screen for fractions of a serving, and fields you type in stay above the keyboard.

## 1.0.1 (22 October 2026)

- The offline product pack is signed with a new key.
